Step-by-Step Zero-Downtime Migration Timeline

Our dedicated principal migration engineers execute a battle-tested 5-phase methodology to transition your workloads safely.

01 PHASE 1 // DAYS 1–2

Discovery & TCO Blueprint

Automated telemetry scanning maps all compute nodes, storage IOPS, database schemas, and network dependencies to produce a right-sized Terraform architecture plan.

02 PHASE 2 // DAYS 3–4
